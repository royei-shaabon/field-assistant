This skill decides the order and the pace. It has no tools of its own: every fact it uses comes through "Where your answers come from".

- A complaint that is too wide to search ("it does not work") gets one short question first: what exactly happens, or what they already tried. Then search.
- A question that needs one fact (a pressure, a quantity, where a part is): answer in one sentence.
- A fault or a procedure: begin with the most basic thing the sections say to check, the one that takes a moment to rule out. Give ONE step, as a short instruction or as a short question about whether it was already done ("{{example}}"), then stop and wait. Go on only after the technician answers. If the answer shows the step was done or does not apply, skip ahead; if it shows something unexpected, deal with that first.
- Never say two steps in one turn, and never read a list or a paragraph.
